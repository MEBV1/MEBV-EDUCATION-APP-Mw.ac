About Malawi Education Books and Vacancies

Malawi Education is a digital learning and careers platform supporting students, teachers and professionals with books, video lessons, Python learning, and job opportunities.

Our Mission

To create an accessible, curriculum-focused digital learning environment that empowers Malawian learners, educators, and early career professionals.

Our Vision

A Malawi where every student has access to relevant books, training resources and career guidance through a modern, trusted online platform.

What We Offer

  • Curriculum-aligned textbooks and past exam papers
  • Video lessons for core subjects and professional development
  • Python Academy with lessons, quizzes, and certificates
  • Job vacancy listings with save and apply tools
  • Service requests and support for learners

Trusted Education Partners

Our platform connects learners with trusted digital learning material and updates from Malawi's education community.

Community Impact

We support learners across MSCE, JCE, primary and professional training by making resources available on mobile-friendly pages.

Our Values

Accessibility, quality, trust, and student success are at the heart of every service we provide.
